About IBVAP

Built to turn passive video feeds into operational intelligence.

IBVAP focuses on extracting actionable events from camera infrastructure while keeping command workflows clear, controlled and responsive for security teams.

◎

Our objective

Improve situational awareness by combining AI-assisted video analysis, identity workflows, event-driven alerting and operator-focused command tools into one coherent surveillance platform. The project is aligned with SIH 2026 Problem Statement SIH26187 for the Ministry of Home Affairs / Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB).

⚡

Operational philosophy

Detect locally where possible, transmit meaningful events, keep workflows permission-aware and preserve evidence for review.

▦

For field & command teams

Separate privileges help administrators, supervisors, operators and auditors focus on the information relevant to their role.

Core features

Detection, context and response in one surveillance stack.

Computer vision, identity intelligence, alerting, evidence and access control are combined into a single operational platform.

01

Unified Camera Intelligence

Connect existing CCTV and compatible camera feeds, organize them operationally and surface live previews centrally.

02

AI Edge Analytics

YOLO/ONNX-based detection designed for GPU-accelerated edge inference and reduced cloud-side video processing.

03

Identity-Aware Tracking

Combine person detection, tracking IDs and face-verification workflows across camera handoffs.

04

Real-Time Alerting

Trigger operational events for unknown-person activity, line crossing, zones and other configured surveillance conditions.

05

Role-Based Access

Restrict users to the feeds and functions assigned to their operational responsibilities.

06

Tactical Command View

Bring camera state, incidents, analytics and map-based situational awareness into one C2-oriented interface.

Architecture

From camera feed to command decision.

A hybrid edge-and-server architecture keeps video intelligence close to the source while data, permissions and workflows remain centrally managed.

01

Input Layer

Existing CCTV, laptop/web cameras and compatible IP camera streams.

02

Edge AI Layer

Detection, tracking and local inference with optimized AI models and CUDA-capable hardware.

03

API & Event Layer

FastAPI coordinates users, cameras, detection events, alerts and integrations.

04

Data Layer

PostgreSQL for durable data and Redis for fast-changing tracking and alert state.

05

Command Layer

React-based operational UI for operators, supervisors and administrators.

Security & control

Access is part of the architecture.

Camera visibility can be enforced server-side based on user role and assignment, preventing non-admin users from accessing feeds outside their responsibility.
